the thing i keep coming back to is that dorian never actually changes. the painting does. he gets to stay the version of himself everyone likes, and somewhere upstairs, locked in a room, there's a record of everything he's really been doing.

## what it says

dorian is young and good looking, a painter named basil paints his portrait, and lord henry spends the whole book telling him that youth and beauty are the only things worth having. dorian wishes the painting would age instead of him, and it does. every cruel thing he does shows up on the canvas, not on his face. by the end the painting is unbearable to look at, and when he finally tries to destroy it, he's the one who dies.

what got me is that wilde doesn't make the painting magic in an interesting way. it's just honest. it's the only thing in the book that shows dorian what he is, and he hides it because he can't stand to see it.

> every portrait that is painted with feeling is a portrait of the artist, not of the sitter.

## where it collides with everything else

i think everyone has a version of the portrait now, it's just flipped. the thing we show people is the clean version, and the real record is somewhere we don't look at.

- in basketball, a highlight reel is the face and the game film is the portrait. the reel makes you look great. the film shows every time you got lazy on defence. you only get better by watching the film.
- with guitar it's the same. the part people hear is the one song you can play cleanly. the part that matters is the hours of practice that sound bad.
- a github contribution graph, or honestly a portfolio site like this one, is a face. it shows the good days. it doesn't show the projects i abandoned or the code i'm not proud of.

lord henry is also interesting to me because he never does anything himself. he just talks, and dorian acts on it. it made me think about how much of what i believe i've picked up from people who sound confident, without checking whether they live by it.

## what i'm doing differently

i want to keep looking at my own game film, even when the highlight reel is more fun to watch.
