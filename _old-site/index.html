About

I'm Ved. I grew up in Calgary, came to Waterloo for math, and I keep ending up on projects where the code has to survive contact with the real world.

Right now that means writing sensor drivers for WARG's drone autopilot, where a bad pointer isn't a stack trace, it's a crash. At SPAIS I'm building the eyes for a strawberry-farm robot. And on the side I run the Youth Policy Council's website, by myself, from the first wireframe to the 190th test.

Before any of that, I spent most of high school in Model UN, eventually running our conference as secretary-general. It left me with two habits: reading the news like box scores, and a soft spot for problems where the hard part is people, not code.

Away from the keyboard it's pickup basketball, guitar, anime, and philosophy, mostly the Stoics. Seneca's line up top is the one I come back to most. And Spider-Man has always been my favourite hero, which is why this page swings.
