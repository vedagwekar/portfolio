A shelf is a list. I wanted somewhere the books could talk to each other.

Most of what I read lands in one of three piles: how machines work, how minds work, and
how people should live. The piles keep leaking into each other. A paper on reinforcement
learning reads like Epictetus on what is and isn't in your control. A datasheet for a
barometer is a small lesson in humility about what you can actually measure.

So this is half review, half braindump. Each entry starts from a book (or just an idea),
and then wanders wherever it collides with the rest: code, models, the Stoics, the news.
The **threads** at the bottom link entries that argue with each other.

None of it is finished. That's the point.
